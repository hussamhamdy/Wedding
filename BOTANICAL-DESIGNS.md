## Artwork

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. Final website asset: `classic/assets/images/botanical-corner.png` (transparent PNG). The supplied invitations were used as style references.

Final asset prompt:

Create a botanical watercolor floral CORNER ORNAMENT asset for a luxury wedding website, using the two attached invitations as style references only. No words, letters, numbers, frames, paper, or background. A lush elegant arrangement coming from the TOP LEFT corner, leaving bottom right as empty transparent space. Large warm white ivory dahlias, small white blossoms with ochre centers, layered olive green eucalyptus and olive leaves, exquisite curling antique gold leaf sprigs, graceful cascading stems. Richly detailed fine art watercolor stationery illustration, muted natural tones, delicate edges, premium printed botanical style, NOT clipart. Square canvas, artwork in upper and left halves with some diagonal center flowers, lower right fully transparent. Genuine transparent background.
